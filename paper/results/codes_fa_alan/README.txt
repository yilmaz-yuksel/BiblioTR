Manual codes, keyed by TR Dizin record identifier (https://search.trdizin.gov.tr/tr/yayin/detay/<id>).
Titles, abstracts, author names, institutions, reference texts and free-text notes are left out because
TR Dizin's terms do not allow records to be redistributed. Rows of links_V4, references_V4 and
references_recheck_V4 follow the fixed-seed order of paper/scripts/11_reference_coding.py.
citation_recall: `code` is the final judgement used in the paper; `first_pass` is the coder's first pass,
before a review of the sheet (see paper/protocol/04_validation_protocol_20261002.md).
Codes: 1 = yes / correct / relevant, 0 = no, NA = not applicable or undecided, reference = the first
identity of an author group.
